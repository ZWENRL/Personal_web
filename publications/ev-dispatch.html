Industrial Engineering Journal, 29(5), 48–58 (2026). Published in Chinese. Journal article and DOI.

We developed an event-triggered policy based on proximal policy optimization for a charging station with stochastic vehicle arrivals and departures. The numerical experiments compare aggregator revenue against benchmark scheduling algorithms.
