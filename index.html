  • I am currently a master’s student in Logistics Engineering and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

  • My research focuses on operations research for electrified transportation and energy systems. I study vehicle-to-grid scheduling and pricing using mathematical programming, discrete-choice models, stochastic simulation, and reinforcement learning.
  • My first-author manuscript on V2G charging-station pricing is under review at Transportation Research Part E. My ongoing work addresses electric bus scheduling and bidirectional charging. Research outputs.
