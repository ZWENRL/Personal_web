About Me

I am a master’s student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I also earned my B.Eng. in Industrial Engineering. I spent a semester as an exchange student at KTH Royal Institute of Technology.

I study how electric transportation can support the power grid, from scheduling electric bus fleets to designing prices for charging stations.

Research Focus

Electric bus fleets. I am developing an optimization model and a branch-and-price prototype to coordinate bus duties and bidirectional charging while accounting for service reliability and battery degradation.

Charging-station operations. My work uses discrete-choice models, simulation, and reinforcement learning to study pricing and real-time dispatch. My first-author manuscript on V2G charging-station pricing is under review at Transportation Research Part E.
